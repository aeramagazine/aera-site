our mission

through a fashion lens

We hope to bring awareness to different aspects of society through a fashion lens, with a focus on cultural, political and environmental issues. Along the way, we want to amplify student voices and encourage people to be untypical.

magazine every semester

what are we creating?

We publish one magazine per semester, and each issue includes several stories from each of our three sections: fashion & culture, fashion & politics and fashion & environment.

Beyond work time during meetings, we host guest speaker lectures, fun activities, lessons on current events and much more.

In short, we’re creating a space for you to have fun while curating something sophisticated and intentional.
