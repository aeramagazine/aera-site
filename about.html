our mission

through a fashion lens

We want to bring awareness to different parts of society through a fashion lens, with a focus on cultural, political and environmental issues. Along the way, we want to amplify student voices and encourage people to be untypical.

magazine every semester

what are we creating?

We publish one magazine each semester. Every issue has stories from our three sections: fashion and culture, fashion and politics, and fashion and environment.

Meetings are for working on the magazine, and we also host guest speakers, talks on current events and other activities.
